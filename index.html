  • /ˌkɑrkəˈraɪnɪdz/

  • /ˈlæmnɪdz/

    The mackerel sharks — white sharks and makos.

  • /ˈdɛntɪkəlz/

Slide 2The eyesocket and lid

  • /ˈɔrbɪt/

    Another word for the eye socket.

  • /ˈnɪktɪteɪtɪŋ ˈmɛmbreɪn/

    A clear third eyelid that covers the eye and still lets the animal see.

    The slide spells it “nicitating” — the standard spelling is nictitating.

On an iPhone, the side switch mutes this like it mutes a video — if a word looks like it played but you heard nothing, flip the ringer back on or turn the volume up.

Pronunciations follow the dictionary entries where they exist — elasmobranch, pelagic, denticle, orbit and nictitating membrane. The three family adjectives — carcharhinid, sphyrnid, lamnid — aren’t in dictionaries; those follow the usual way biologists anglicize the genus names Carcharhinus, Sphyrna and Lamna.
